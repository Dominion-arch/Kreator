# Kreator
Kreator is a video creator platform built to give content creators an all-in-one space to produce, organize, and publish their video content — without needing to juggle multiple disconnected tools. It's designed with the modern creator's workflow in mind: taking raw ideas and footage through editing, organization, and distribution in one streamlined experience.
